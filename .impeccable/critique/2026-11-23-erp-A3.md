## Design specificity verdict

Authored, not interchangeable. The Tidy Counter look is carried through: one lime selection, ink primary, pastel
figure tiles, Bangla set at readable size, a phone tab bar built from the four counter jobs. Three things read as
generic or assembled instead of designed:

1. **The page head.** Breadcrumb line + h1 with the same words + help chip + fold arrow is stamped on every screen. It is
   an ERP-template habit, costs 55-60 px of a 844 px phone, and says nothing the tab bar / menu does not.
2. **KPI strip on every screen**, including screens whose figures are all zero (Repairs, Print jobs, Campaigns: four
   tiles of "0"). The hero-metric template applied by reflex; on an empty module it is pure decoration.
3. **The Home hub** (six coloured app tiles on an empty canvas) is an Odoo launcher, not this product. On PC it repeats
   the side menu beside it and leaves 80% of the screen blank.

## Cognitive load

Checklist failures: 4 of 8 (high on the worst screens, moderate overall).

- **Single focus - fail.** Dashboard opens on a content-posts card; Expenses (phone) and Dashboard (phone) show two or
  three buttons for the same job.
- **Chunking / minimal choices - fail.** Inbox: 14 tabs + 8 channel icons + 4 piles = 26 visible choices. Products: 2
  page tabs + 5 category tabs + 3 view segments + sort + filter + columns + density + export. Settings: 11 tabs.
  CRM tools: 8 tabs wrapping to four rows on phone.
- **Visual hierarchy - fail** on Profit (one number three times at three sizes), Cash (two "in hand" totals),
  Stock entry PC (card title centred over left-aligned content).
- **Progressive disclosure - fail** on Print jobs, Resell, CRM tools: an explanatory paragraph sits above the work.
- Pass: grouping, one-thing-at-a-time (Invoice, Stock entry stepper), working memory (summary stays beside the invoice
  on PC), phone "সারাংশ ৪টি সংখ্যা" fold.

## What is working

1. **Phone Customers and phone Sales rows carry the decision in the row**: amount right, due chip in red with age
   ("৳7,800 বাকি · ১১৫ দিন"). A counter clerk can answer "does he owe us" without opening anything.
2. **New invoice** is a real counter tool: search first, sticky bar that tells the next step in words, save disabled
   until there is a line, summary pinned on PC.
3. **Shipments "cost not written" notice** states the size of the problem (609 units, ৳12,21,316), the consequence (not
   in profit) and one button. This is the trust principle in PRODUCT.md done properly.

## Per-screen findings

Severity: P1 major, P2 minor, P3 polish.

### 1. home
- **P2 - PC hub repeats the side menu.** Six tiles (`.o_apps` / `.o_tile`) float in an empty 1200 px canvas next to a
  menu that already lists the same places. The owner at a PC lands on a screen with no figure and no task. Fix: on
  desktop (`min-width:761px`) open the dashboard instead of `homeHtml()`, or put "এখন যা দেখতে হবে" under the tiles
  (logic: route change in the home view).
- **P2 - tile words differ from the menu.** Tile "কন্টাক্ট" opens what the menu calls "কাস্টমার"; tile "ইনভেন্টরি" is
  the menu group "মাল" / "স্টক". One word per idea is a PRODUCT.md rule. Fix: change the `APPS` labels / `I18N_BN`
  (`'Contacts': 'কাস্টমার'`, Inventory → 'স্টক').
- P3 - phone: fine. Tiles are 66 px, labels readable.

### 2. dash
- **P1 - the dashboard opens on social posts, not money.** The first card on both sizes is "এই সপ্তাহের পোস্ট"
  (`section.cd.cdh`), 270 px tall on PC and 390 px on phone. On phone, "এখন যা দেখতে হবে" (3 parcels to send, 18 out of
  stock) starts at y=630 and sales figures are below the fold. The owner "judges by the first screen"; staff do not
  post content. Fix: move `.cdh` under the KPI grid / attention list (insertion order where the dash view is wrapped),
  or collapse it to its one-line footer ("এ সপ্তাহে গেছে ০ · যাবে ০ · অনুমোদনের অপেক্ষায় ১") until opened.
- **P2 - phone has three "new invoice" entries in one viewport**: the black full-width "+ নতুন ইনভয়েস" bar, the "+" in
  the top bar and the "ইনভয়েস" tab. Fix: drop the bar on phone (`.nt-dash-acts` (confirm)) - the tab bar already owns
  that job.
- **P2 - stray "i" above the sales card title** (PC, at the top edge of "এ মাসের বিক্রি", x≈436 y≈460): the info button
  (`.fdd-i`) sits outside its tile's corner, detached from the figure it explains. Fix: for the chart card place
  `.fdd-i` in the head row after the title, not absolutely at top.
- P3 - "9:00 PM" in the post row is a Latin-digit time; rule says times in Bangla digits ("রাত ৯:০০"). Phone: the
  period row cuts "কাস্টমাইজ করুন" to a pencil.

### 3. contentdesk
- **P2 - title printed three times**: crumb "আজ / কনটেন্ট ডেস্ক", then "Demo 3D Shop · কনটেন্ট ডেস্ক", with the lime
  menu item beside it. On phone that is 150 px before the first tab. Fix: hide `#o_cp .crumbs` when the view prints
  its own h1, or drop the in-view title.
- **P2 - the main button looks dead.** "অনুমোদন · রুটিনের সময়ে যাবে" is a mid-grey fill with white text (about 2.5:1),
  read as broken, and the reason ("কোথায় যাবে, অন্তত একটা বাছুন") is in small grey under it while Facebook already
  looks ticked. Fix: disabled `.cd-btn` = Shelf Grey fill + Ink-3 text (the system's 45% rule), and move the reason
  line above the buttons in warning-text colour.
- P3 - phone: "কনটেন্ট ইঞ্জিন চালু" status and "নতুন করে আনুন" share a row with unequal heights (38 vs 42 px).

### 4. invoice
- **P2 - fold arrows are unlabeled and crowd the "?"** (PC): each card title has "?" then a boxed "▲" 6 px apart, both
  ~24 px. A new clerk cannot tell what "▲" does, and on a counter PC it is an easy mis-click that hides the product
  search. Fix: fold button gets `aria-label` + title "ভাঁজ করুন", moves to the card's right edge, min 32 px; the three
  cards a clerk always needs (search, lines, customer) should not fold at all.
- **P2 - summary mixes "0" and "৳0"**: উপমোট / ছাড় / ডেলিভারি / এখনই পেমেন্ট show "0", মোট and বাকি show "৳0". Money
  should look the same everywhere. Fix: format every row with `tk()`.
- **P3 - phone "?" buttons are 24 px circles** next to titles (4 in the first viewport). DESIGN.md asks 40 px tap area;
  confirm the hit area is padded (`::after{inset:-8px}`) as was done for `.fdd-ti`.
- Otherwise clean: sticky bar text changes with the device ("ট্যাপ করে বেছে নিন" on phone).

### 5. holds (কনফার্মের অপেক্ষায়)
- **P2 - on phone the selected lime tab is off-screen.** The row shows "সব ইনভয়েস · ওয়েব অর্ডার · কোটেশন" and the

  selected. Fix: after paint call `scrollIntoView({inline:'center',block:'nearest'})` on the `[aria-selected="true"]`

- **P2 - three names for one place**: menu "অপেক্ষার অর্ডার", page "কনফার্মের অপেক্ষায়", sub-tab "অপেক্ষায়". Fix: one
  label in nav and title.
- P3 - "বন্ধ" is the only sub-tab without a count; empty state is good (says when it fills and when it is not needed).
