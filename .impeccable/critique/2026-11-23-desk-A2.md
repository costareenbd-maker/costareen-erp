Date: 23 Nov 2026. Method: design review only (Assessment A; no detector run, no other critique file read).
Surface: `web/app.html`, Brand desk (`.bc` in `#bc_root`), pages: linkedin, results, biz, routine, rules, aud, train, map.
Evidence: the 16 screenshots in `.impeccable/review/desk-all/` (`pc-*.png` 1440 wide, `phone-*.png` 390 wide), plus
`pc-home.png` / `pc-plan.png` for consistency; class names and CSS read from the desk blocks (`brand-13` .. `whole-2`).

## Design specificity verdict

Mostly authored for this product. The network line colours on rails, rings and badges, the Bangla day-part routine
("সকাল / দুপুর ও বিকেল / রাত"), the LinkedIn daily caps with a kill switch, and the Connections map are things no generic
dashboard has. Two places fall back to category defaults: Results (stat tiles, then a table of mostly empty cells) and
Business & sites (metric blocks per site). The move into the ERP shell today is half-finished in the detail: lime was
applied to every `.bc-pills button.on` at once, so the ERP rule "one lime row per screen" is broken on five of these
eight pages, and one phone control now has white text on lime. The structure is right; the finish is not there yet.

## Findings per page

### 1. LinkedIn (`pc-linkedin.png`, `phone-linkedin.png`)

- **[P2] The page's most prominent control is the emergency stop.** `button.bc-btn.redline` ("LinkedIn থামান") is the
  only coloured thing in the header, red-outlined, beside the page title on both sizes; on the phone it is the first
  thing under the thumb at the top. It is used perhaps once a year, yet it outweighs "AI দিয়ে লিখুন", the daily action.
  Fix: move it to the foot of the page as a `.bc-btn.ghost.sm` with red text (or into the `.li-health` row); keep the
  in-page confirm (`.bc-call`) as is. Small markup move in `liHtml()`.
- **[P2] "এ সপ্তাহে পোস্ট ২২/৩" shows no alarm.** In `.li-health` the count is 22 against a limit of 3 and is drawn
  like the calm numbers beside it. Either the limit is wrong or he is seven times over; the owner cannot tell. Fix
  (one line of logic): add class `x` to that `<span>` when `posts_week > 3` (the row already styles `.li-health .x`),
  or drop the "/৩" if posts made by the routine are not meant to count against it.
- **[P2] "কমেন্ট আনুন" does not say what it does.** The `.bc-btn.ghost` beside "AI দিয়ে লিখুন" pulls comments on his
  own posts from LinkedIn; the label reads as "bring a comment" with no object. Fix: label "আমার পোস্টের নতুন কমেন্ট
  আনুন", and move it out of the form's action row (`.uc-a`) to the "আজকের কাজ" heading (`.bc-h2`) where its result lands.
- **[P3] The warm-up note looks like a disabled field.** `p.li-warm` ("১১ ডিসেম্বর পর্যন্ত অর্ধেক সীমা চলছে") is a grey
  rounded box the size and colour of an input. The same style (`.tr-empty`) is used for the empty state lower down.
  Fix: `.li-warm{background:none;padding:0}` with a 9px amber dot in front (it is a "waiting" state), and say why:
  "নতুন অ্যাকাউন্ট গরম হচ্ছে, তাই ...".
- **[P3] Phone: the stop button and refresh crowd the title.** `phone-linkedin.png` top row: title, an icon-only
  refresh, then the red button. Moving the stop button (first finding) fixes this too.
- **[P3] Meters.** `.li-m` for "রিপ্লাই" has no bar and a different height of content, so the four tiles do not align
  ("সীমা নেই" sits where the bar is in the others). Fix: render an empty `.li-bar` at 0 opacity to hold the line.

### 2. Results / ফলাফল (`pc-results.png`, `phone-results.png`)

- **[P1] The post table is a wall of dashes, and on the phone it is 4,000px of it.** `.rs-list` shows 20 rows x 5
  number columns; in the screenshot 15 of 20 rows are all "—", and the "রিচ" and "শেয়ার" columns are "—" in every row
  including the total. On the phone each `.rs-row` becomes a ~180px card with five labelled dashes and a "খুলুন" link,
  so the two useful blocks ("সেরা পোস্ট", "বিষয় ধরে") sit after about 4,300px of scrolling. The owner on his phone will
  never reach them. Fix: (a) order - put `ol.rs-top` and `.rs-pils` above `.rs-list` (move two blocks in
  `resultsHtml()`); (b) hide a column whose total is null: add a class on `.rs-list` per empty key and
  `.rs-list.no-reach [data-k="রিচ"]{display:none}` (small logic); (c) rows with no numbers collapse to one line:
  `.rs-row.nonum .rs-n{display:none}` plus one grey "সংখ্যা আসেনি" tag; (d) phone: show 5 rows before "আরও".
- **[P2] The meaning of "—" is explained only at the bottom of the page.** "— মানে নেটওয়ার্ক সংখ্যাটা দেয়নি" is the last
  line (`.bc-note`), 3,100px down on PC and 5,500px on the phone. Fix: put that sentence in the `.bc-h2 span` of
  "পোস্ট ধরে" (it currently says only "নতুন আগে").
- **[P2] Five of seven networks are "মাপা যায় না", shown at the same weight as real results.** `ul.rs-nom` takes five
  full rows. It is honest, but two of the reasons are things he can fix ("সংখ্যা আনার পথ এখনো যুক্ত হয়নি") and three are
  not, and they look identical. Fix: fold the list behind one line ("৫টা নেটওয়ার্ক মাপা যায় না - কেন") using the
  existing `.bc-fold`, and give the two fixable ones a `.bc-btn.sm` to the connections page.
- **[P2] "খুলুন" is violet here and nowhere else on the desk.** `a.bc-btn.ghost.sm` inside `.rs-a` measures #5748E6
  (the ERP link colour leaking onto an anchor); every other ghost button on these pages is ink. Fix:
  `.bc a.bc-btn{color:inherit}` (or `.rs-a .bc-btn{color:var(--bc-ink)}`). On the phone the link also has nothing that
  says it leaves the desk; add the `out` icon used on "সাইট খুলুন".
- **[P3] Two network tiles fill half the width.** `.rs-tiles` holds 2 tiles at ~275px and leaves 580px empty on PC,
  while the trend deltas inside (`+৫৩৪`) are about 10-11px. Fix: `grid-template-columns:repeat(auto-fit,minmax(280px,1fr))`
  with a max of 3, and raise the delta text to 12.5px.
- **[P3] Mixed digits.** `ol.rs-top` numbers its rows "1 2 3" in Latin (the browser's list counter) beside Bangla
  figures. Fix: `list-style:none` plus a Bangla counter, or drop the numbers.
- **[P3] Duplicate-looking rows.** Several posts appear twice with the same first line (short and long version, e.g.
  "একজন seller জিজ্ঞেস করলেন..."), one with numbers and one without. It reads as a bug. Needs logic: group versions
  of one idea under one row.

### 3. Business & sites / ব্যবসা ও সাইট (`pc-biz.png`, `phone-biz.png`)

- **[P2] Problems are stated with no way to act.** `.bc-wait.calm.sx-umami` says "মোবাইলের গতি মাপা যাচ্ছে না: Google
  PageSpeed-এর চাবি Mac-এ বসানো বাকি" and the third site says "সাইটে tracker বসানো বাকি". Both are grey boxes that look
  like disabled fields, both use tool words he does not know, and neither has a button. Result: "গতি (মোবাইল) —" on all
  three sites forever. Fix: one line each in plain words plus a `.bc-btn.sm` ("কীভাবে বসাবেন" opening the help sheet, or
  a link to Rules > "Mac-এর চাবি"). If only the developer can do it, say "ডেভেলপারের কাজ" so he stops reading it as his.
- **[P2] "ভাঙা লিংক ০ / ২৫" is ambiguous.** In `.sx-facts` the value reads "0 of 25" - 0 broken out of 25 checked, but
  it can be read as a score. The third site shows "০ / ১". Fix: text "০টা ভাঙা" with `<small>২৫টা দেখা হয়েছে</small>`.
- **[P2] Raw web addresses as content.** "বেশি দেখা পাতা" lists `/brand/sunlu/`, `/` and "কোথা থেকে এলেন" lists
  `lm.facebook.com`, `l.facebook.com`, `m.facebook.com`, `facebook.com` as separate sources. For a trader, all four
  are "Facebook" and "/" is "হোম পেজ". Needs small logic: map `/` to "হোম পেজ" and merge `*.facebook.com` into Facebook.
- **[P2] Phone: the two "তালিকা বদলান" buttons become a bare pencil.** `.bc-btn.ghost.sm.sx-lb` hides its `<span>` under
  759px. Two identical unlabeled pencils (one next to "এখন পরীক্ষা করুন", one next to "ব্যবসা") - he must tap to learn
  what they do. Fix: keep the label on the phone (`.sx-lb span{display:inline}`; there is room on the "ব্যবসা" row) or
  shorten to "বদলান".
- **[P3] Two labels for "go to the ERP".** `.sx-biz` rows: "ERP খুলুন ↗" (new tab, own login) and "ERP-তে যান ›" (same
  tab). The difference is real but is explained only in the footnote. Fix: put the difference in the button:
  "নতুন ট্যাবে খুলুন" and "এখানেই খুলুন".
- **[P3] "পরিসংখ্যান" does not say where it goes.** `a.bc-btn.ghost.sm` opens stats.costareen.com in a new tab with no
  external icon, unlike "সাইট খুলুন" beside it. Add the same `out` icon and label "পুরো পরিসংখ্যান".

### 4. Routine / রুটিন (`pc-routine.png`, `phone-routine.png`)

- **[P1] Phone: white text on lime.** In `.rt-days` the selected day ("সোম") shows its sub-line "আজ · ১০" at 1.08:1
  (measured #F8FFD6 on #E4FF5A). Cause: unity-1 turned `.rt-days button.on` lime but `.rt-days button.on small
  {color:#fff;opacity:.75}` from brand-17 still applies. This also breaks DESIGN.md's "never white on lime". Fix (CSS,
  one line): `.rt-days button.on small{color:#17181C;opacity:.72}`.
- **[P1] PC: seven columns at 1440 make every slot hard to read.** `.rt-week` gives each `.rt-day` about 150px.
  A slot title wraps to two lines ("নিজের একটা / ছবি"), the network and topic wrap to two more, and the status line
  `.rt-dep .fill` is cut after 8-10 characters ("যাবে: ক্রেতা প্রা…", "অপেক্ষায়: Ban…") - the one line that says which
  post goes in that slot is unreadable in all ~70 slots. Meta text is 12px, under the 14px Bangla floor. Fix (CSS):
  `.rt-dep{grid-template-columns:1fr}` with `time` on the same line as the title (frees 40px), `.rt-dep .p{font-size:13px}`,
  `.rt-dep .fill{white-space:normal;display:-webkit-box;-webkit-line-clamp:2;-webkit-box-orient:vertical}`. Or raise
  the single-day breakpoint from `@container bc (max-width:1180px)` to about 1500px so a 1440 screen gets the roomy
  one-day view with the day strip.
- **[P2] Two lime rows on the phone, and the day strip competes with the level switch.** `.rt-ctl .bc-pills` ("হালকা /
  মাঝারি / পুরো") and `.rt-days` are both lime, 150px apart (plus the lime menu on PC). Fix: lime stays on the day
  strip (the page's main selection); the level switch takes the neutral selected style
  (`.rt-ctl .bc-pills button.on{background:var(--fd-surface-3,#E7E8EC);color:#17181C;box-shadow:inset 0 0 0 1px #D7D9DF}`).
- **[P2] The network chips do not say what they are.** `.rt-nets .st-net` (Facebook, Instagram ...) are filters, but
  there is no label, and "none chosen = all shown" looks the same as "all chosen". On the phone the row is cut at
  "Y…" with no fade or arrow. Fix: a `.rt-k` label "শুধু দেখুন:" before the row, an "সব" chip first, and a right-edge
  fade mask on `.rt-nets` under 759px.
- **[P2] The red tick means "target", but red means "stuck" on this desk.** `.rt-bar u` (the target mark on the
  Facebook topic bars) is red; so is the alert colour. Four red marks under "Facebook-এ বিষয়ের ভাগ" read as four
  problems. Fix: `.rt-bar u{background:var(--bc-ink)}`, 2px wide, taller than the bar.
- **[P3] "শুরুর রুটিনে ফিরুন" is a quiet ghost button that wipes his routine edits.** It does confirm in the page
  (good), but sits alone at the foot, styled like a harmless link. Fix: red text (`color:var(--bc-alert-ink)`) and the
  label "সব বদল মুছে শুরুর রুটিনে ফিরুন".
- **[P3] Link style.** "তারিখ ধরে পোস্ট → পরিকল্পনা" (`.pn-link`) is an underlined ink link; fine, but on the phone it
  is 36px tall (`min-height:36px`). Raise to 44px.

### 5. Rules / নিয়ম (`pc-rules.png`, `phone-rules.png`)

- **[P1] The topic-mix bars cannot be read.** `.bc-mixbar` shades seven topics in a grey ramp (`TSH`); the legend
  `.bc-tlg` shows seven grey squares of which the last three are near-identical light greys. Segments under 20% carry
  no label (Instagram's second segment is a blank dark block), and labels are cut to the first word ("কে-বিউটি ৩০%").
  The line under each bar (`p.bc-mixt`) then repeats everything in text, in a different order from the bar. So the
  bar adds nothing and the page is twice as long (6 networks x 2). Fix: keep one. Simplest: drop `.bc-mixbar` and
  `.bc-tlg`, and render `p.bc-mixt` as a small list sorted by share, each with a thin ink bar (the `.rs-pils` pattern
  from Results). If the stacked bar stays: use four ink steps only, hatch or outline the rest, and label every segment
  of 10% or more.
- **[P2] Two lime rows on one page.** `.bc-h2 .bc-pills` ("শুরু · ~৬ ঘণ্টা") and `.mw-keep .bc-pills` ("৩০ দিন") are
  both lime, plus the lime menu pill. Neither is a "where you are" selection; both are settings. Fix: both take the
  neutral selected style (same CSS as in Routine above), so lime stays on the menu only.
- **[P2] "শুরু · ~৬ ঘণ্টা / বাড়ানো · ~১০ ঘণ্টা" does not say six hours of what.** It is his own time per week at two
  workload levels; the only explanation is a `title` tooltip on the second button (never seen on a phone). Fix: a
  label before the pills: "আপনার সময়, সপ্তাহে:" and labels "কম (৬ ঘণ্টা)" / "বেশি (১০ ঘণ্টা)".
- **[P2] "অ্যাকাউন্ট নেই" is a dead end, and rules exist for accounts that do not.** `.bc-tag.ol` on Instagram and
  Threads appears four times (targets and mixes), each time with full targets beside it. Fix: make the tag a button
  (`.bc-btn.sm`) "যুক্ত করুন" to the connection screen, and dim the row (`.bc-cad`, `.bc-mix` at 55%) until connected.
- **[P2] "বন্ধ / চালু" in "পোস্ট যাওয়ার পথ" look like switches but are not.** `.bc-con .bc-tag` sits at the right edge
  exactly where the desk's toggles sit on the Train page; six of seven say "বন্ধ" and nothing can be pressed. He will
  tap them. Fix: move the tag next to the name (left), and add one line under the heading: "এগুলো ডেভেলপার চালু করে".
- **[P3] The target rings have no key.** `.bc-dots i` (filled = minimum, hollow = up to the maximum) is not explained;
  the number "৫–৭টা" at the far right, 700px away from the rings on PC, says the same thing. Fix: put the number right
  after the rings (`.bc-cad b{margin-left:12px;justify-self:start}`).
- **[P3] "Mac-এর চাবি (বসানো আছে)"** at the foot is developer wording; "সার্ভারের চাবি" or hide it when set.

### 6. Audience / দর্শক (`pc-aud.png`, `phone-aud.png`)

- **[P1] Column heads and card titles fade to unreadable.** The audience ramp colour is used as TEXT colour:
  `.au-mh span{color:var(--c)}` and `.au-ch b{color:var(--c)}`. Measured: "ব্র্যান্ড ও সাপ্লায়ার" 3.21:1 (#8A909B),
  "বিদেশি বায়ার" 1.97:1 (#B4B9C1) - both as a 12px column head and as the 16px card title. Two of four audiences look
  switched off, though they hold 50% of LinkedIn. Fix (CSS): `.au-mh span,.au-ch b{color:var(--bc-ink)}`; the ramp
  stays on the card's top rule (`.au-c{border-top}`) and the cell bar (`.au-cell i`), where it already is. Add a 10px
  ramp square before each head so the link between column and card survives.
- **[P1] The cards are raw English AI text.** `p.au-who` and `dl.au-dl dd` show the stored prompt text: "none: the
  page is an educator. It gives free...", "not used: no sales qualification on the personal brand". The owner reads
  Bangla; this is the one page that tells him WHO the desk writes for, and he cannot check it. DESIGN.md says English
  AI notes sit behind a Bangla fold. Fix (logic, small): show a Bangla one-line summary per card (store `who_bn`), and
  put the English text behind the existing `.bc-fold` ("AI-কে যা বলা আছে (English)"). "অফার: none: ..." should read "নেই".
- **[P2] The share grid has no row total and no check.** `.au-cell input.bc-in` takes any number; each network row
  should add to 100 (65+35, 50+50) but nothing shows the sum, unlike Rules where `.bc-sum` shows "১০০ / ১০০" and
  blocks save. One black "ভাগ সেভ করুন" (`.uc-a .bc-btn.dark`) is always live, changed or not. Fix (small logic): a
  fifth column with the row sum in the `.bc-sum` style (red when not 100), and disable the button until dirty and valid.
- **[P2] Phone: the inputs are 34px tall.** `.au-cell input.bc-in{height:34px}`, 24 of them in a tight grid, ~62px
  wide with 4px gaps; column heads drop to 11px and wrap to two lines. Fix: under 640px `height:44px`, head 12.5px.
- **[P3] "বদলান" is a third pattern here.** On this page it is a chevron fold (`.bc-fold`), on Rules a ghost button
  (`.bc-btn.ghost.sm`), on Business a pencil button. Same word, three looks. Pick the pencil + label form for
  "open an editor" everywhere.
- **[P3] "v৪" beside the title** (`.au-v`) is a version number that means nothing to him. Hide it, or "৪ বার বদলানো".
- **[P3] Latin digits in the grid** (65, 35) while every other count on the desk is Bangla. Inputs are fine to stay
  Latin, but then the hint "০ মানে ..." above should match.

### 7. Writing style / লেখার ধরন (`pc-train.png`, `phone-train.png`)

- **[P2] The six-network grid has no heading and no explanation.** `.tr-board` (Facebook ... Threads, each "সাধারণ")
  sits between two titled sections with no `h2`. "সাধারণ" does not say "no style set for this network yet", and
  nothing shows that a cell is a button (`.tr-row` jumps to the paste box). Fix: add `.bc-h2` "কোন নেটওয়ার্কে কোন ধরন"
  above it; change the empty label to "ধরন নেই · শেখান" with a small chevron.
- **[P2] The next heading is glued to the grid.** "আপনার মন্তব্য থেকে শেখা" starts 8px under `.tr-board`
  (`margin:4px 0 8px`) on PC, while every other section has ~28px above its heading. It reads as the grid's caption.
  Fix: `.tr-board{margin-bottom:28px}`.
- **[P2] Coloured side stripes on the grid.** `.tr-row>i` is a 4px full-height colour stripe on each cell - the
  side-stripe pattern the craft floor rejects, and at the grid's inner edges it sits on top of the cell divider
  (LinkedIn's blue and TikTok's black stripes look like thick column borders). The network logo beside it already
  names the line. Fix: `.tr-row>i{display:none}`.
- **[P2] Switches are ink when on.** `.tr-sw.on i{background:var(--bc-ink)}` (header "ধরন চালু", two profile rows, two
  lesson rows). Now that the desk follows the ERP, where a switch that is on is lime, five black switches and three
