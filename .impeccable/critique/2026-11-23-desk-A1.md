# Brand desk critique - Assessment A (design review), part 1

Date: 23 Nov 2026. Target: `web/app.html`, view `branddesk` (block `brand-13` onwards, restyled by `flow-1`, `unity-1`,
`whole-1`, `whole-2`). Pages reviewed: home, posts, inbox, plan, ideas, media, listen, people - each at 1440 px (PC) and
390 px (phone), from `.impeccable/review/desk-all/` (16 full-page shots, viewed in full and in zoomed crops).
Method: design review only, from screenshots plus the CSS/markup in `app.html`. No detector was run, no other critique
file was read. Nothing was clicked, so states that need a click (sheets, menus, errors, busy, empty) are not judged.
Not judged by instruction: the phone-post previews' own look, people's own words, the content of the Inbox.
Screenshot artefacts ignored: the phone bottom bar drawn mid-page, grey lazy-load thumbnails low on Media/Listen.

Class names below were checked against `app.html`. "Needs logic" marks a fix that is more than CSS / small markup.

## 1. Home (`viewHome`: `.bc-wait`, `.hm-wk`, `.hm-col`, `.hm-c`, `.hm-days`, `.bc-line`)

**H1 [P1] Phone: today's list is drawn on a broken lime band with black dashes down the left edge.**
On the phone the day's cards sit on pale-lime strips that show only in the gaps between cards, and a black line appears
in pieces at the left edge of each gap. Cause: `whole-2` added `.hm-col.today{background:rgba(228,255,90,.2);box-shadow:inset 2px 0 0 #17181C}`
after the phone rule `.hm-col.cur{background:transparent;box-shadow:none;padding:0}`, so the band comes back with no padding.
It reads as a rendering fault on the first screen he opens between meetings.
Fix (CSS): `@container bc (max-width:759px){.hm-col.cur.today{background:transparent;box-shadow:none}}`. The lime day
button in `.hm-days` already says "today".

**H2 [P1] PC: the past pushes the page to 2,850 px and buries the result pills.**
Saturday's column alone holds 26 "গেছে" cards; Sunday 17. The week board is as tall as its longest (oldest) column, so
today's column ends at ~1,300 px and the pills under the board (`.bc-line`: গেছে ৪৫ · যাবে ৪০, প্রশ্ন ৬৩, লিড ১, রুটিন)
sit 2,700 px down under an empty white field. He judges by the first screen; what came back (questions, leads) is not on it.
Fix: fold past days - in `.hm-col.past` show the first 3 `.hm-c` and a `.bc-fold` "আরও N টা গেছে" (small logic in the
column builder), or CSS-only `.hm-col.past{max-height:620px;overflow:auto}`. Move the `.bc-line` row above `.hm-wk`
(markup order) so results are on the first screen.

**H3 [P2] Past cards are faded past the contrast floor.**
`.hm-col.past{opacity:.72}` on top of already-grey text and tinted cards; the green "গেছে" (`.hm-c.go .hm-m b`,
`--bc-good`, 11.5 px, no pill) is the weakest text on the page. Bangla at 11.5 px and reduced opacity breaks the
project's own Bangla Size Rule.
Fix: drop the opacity (`.hm-col.past{opacity:1}`), mute with colour instead (`.hm-col.past .hm-x{color:var(--bc-ink-2)}`),
and set `.hm-m b{font-size:12.5px}`.

**H4 [P2] Wait bar: two buttons, one says a page name.**
"অনুমোদন দিন" (amber) and "পরিকল্পনা" (`.bc-wl`, outline). The second is a noun, not an action, and the sub-line already
says both ("পোস্ট পাতায় দেখে অনুমোদন দিন; পুরো সপ্তাহ পরিকল্পনা পাতায়"). On the phone both are 38 px tall (`.bc-btn` default; only
`.bc-head .bc-btn` got 44).
Fix: label `.bc-wl` "পুরো সপ্তাহ দেখুন"; add `.bc-wait .bc-btn{min-height:44px}`; cut the sub-line to one clause.

**H5 [P2] Status words on the cards are too small and inconsistent in shape.**
"যাবে" is a grey pill, "অপেক্ষায়" an amber pill, "গেছে" bare green text, "ফাঁকা ঘর" / "ভিডিও লাগবে" bare grey/brown text - five
treatments at 11.5 px inside a 150 px card. Reminder rows (`.hm-r`, "১১:৪০ in কমেন্ট · নি...") are cut to "নি..." on PC,
so the row says nothing.
Fix: one tag shape for all (`.hm-m b{padding:2px 7px;border-radius:999px;font-size:12.5px}` for every state, colour by
state); `.hm-r span{white-space:normal}` or shorten the reminder text to "কমেন্টের উত্তর" (copy in the builder).

**H6 [P3] "সময় ঢাকার" hangs as loose grey text.** On the phone it wraps to its own line under the day summary
(`<small>` inside the `আজ:` paragraph). It is a standing fact, not day data. Fix: move it behind the page's "?" help,
or keep it inline with `white-space:nowrap` and a leading " · ".

## 2. Posts (`.bc-pills`, `.ph-jump`, `.ph-grp-h`, `.ph-slot`, `.ph-act`, `.sc-chip`, `.bc-flag`, `.ph-more`)

**P1 [P1] Two different scores on every card.**
The pill under the card says "৯৭ স্কোর" (`.sc-chip`, "সব চেকের স্কোর") and the amber line right below says
"facebook ভাইরাল স্কোর 94/100 (লেখা 89 · হুক 100 · ট্যাগ 100 · কমেন্ট 78 ...)" (`.bc-flag` with `.bc-tag.w` "দেখে নিন"). Same on
every card (92 vs 86, 94 vs 89). He approves on trust in a number; two numbers with the same word, one in Bangla digits
and one in Latin, one calm and one flagged amber, make him stop and wonder which is real - and "দেখে নিন" in amber on a
post scoring 94/100 is a false alarm repeated 32 times.
Fix: needs logic, small. Do not render the viral-score reason as a `.bc-flag` when it is above the pass line; show it
inside the score sheet that `.sc-chip` opens. If both must stay, label the chip "সব চেক ৯৭" and the line "ভাইরাল ৯৪" in the
same digits, and keep the amber tag for real warnings only.

**P2 [P1] Seven controls and two notes under every preview.**
Per card: অনুমোদন দিন, বাদ দিন, লেখা বদলান, ছবি দিন, ৯৭ স্কোর, প্ল্যানের সাথে মিল ৫/৫, আরও, then the amber flag line and
"AI-এর প্রশ্ন (২)". With an image there are two more (অন্য ছবি, নিজে বাছুন). The decision he came for (approve or not) is one
of nine things, and the row order changes between cards with and without a picture.
Fix (markup in the card builder, no new logic): keep row 1 = `অনুমোদন দিন` (dark) + `বাদ দিন` + `লেখা বদলান`; put `.sc-chip`
and the fit chip on one quiet meta row with the "যাবে ..." time; move `ছবি দিন` into the existing "আরও" fold
(`data-bc="more"`). Target: 3 buttons + 1 meta row.

**P3 [P2] "আরও ২২টা আইডিয়ার লেখা দেখুন" is broken and invisible.**
The chevron sits alone on one line with the text under it, in grey, at the very bottom - it is the only door to 22 of
the 25 idea groups. Cause: `.ph-more{all:unset;...}` (written for the "See more" inside phone cards) is reused on
`<button class="bc-fold ph-more">` and wipes `.bc-fold`'s inline-flex. The same class collision does not happen on
Ideas ("আরও ৮৪টা আইডিয়া" renders correctly).
Fix: rename the class on this button (e.g. `bc-fold ph-all`) or add
`.bc-fold.ph-more{display:inline-flex;align-items:center;gap:6px;min-height:44px;color:var(--bc-ink)}`; better, make it
a real `.bc-btn` so it looks like the door it is.

**P4 [P2] Two lime rows side by side read as one control.**
Status tabs (অপেক্ষায় ৩২ | যাবে | গেছে | আটকে) and the network filter (সব ৩২ | LinkedIn | Facebook ...) are two `.bc-pills`
tracks touching each other, each with a lime "on" pill and each showing "৩২". On PC they look like one 9-item bar with
two selections. (Lime for every chosen pill is today's `whole-2` decision; this is about telling the two rows apart.)
Fix: put the network filter on its own line under the tabs (`.bc-head>.bc-pills{flex:1 0 100%}` already exists for the
phone - apply at all widths), or give the second track no shelf background and a "নেটওয়ার্ক:" label.

**P5 [P2] Phone: filter and jump rows scroll sideways with no cue.**
At 390 px the network row ends mid-pill after Facebook (Instagram, TikTok hidden) and the idea jump strip
(`.ph-jump`) ends mid-word; both hide their scrollbar (`scrollbar-width:none`). He will not know TikTok is there.
Fix: fade the right edge: `.bc-pills,.ph-jump{-webkit-mask-image:linear-gradient(90deg,#000 calc(100% - 28px),transparent)}`
while more content remains, or let `.bc-pills` wrap on the phone (`flex-wrap:wrap;border-radius:18px`).

**P6 [P3] Small inconsistencies.** Fit chip shows "8/8" (Latin) on LinkedIn cards and "৫/৫" (Bangla) on Facebook cards -
counts should be Bangla digits everywhere (`pnFitChip`). "যাবে রবি ১৩ ডিসেম্বর, ১২:৩০ সকাল" beside "১২:৩০ দুপুর" on another
card: if that slot is 00:30 the word should be "রাত" (check the day-part function; not verified from the shot). On PC the
right third of the sheet is empty beside two 340 px cards - acceptable, but the group header's "সবগুলোতে অনুমোদন দিন (২)"
sits 700 px from the cards it acts on; align it left after the title.

## 3. Inbox (layout only) (`.bc-pills.it-tabs`, `.bx-tools`, `.bx-nt`, thread rows, reading pane)

**I1 [P2] PC: two thirds of the sheet is an empty reading pane.**
With nothing open, the list is a 360 px column and the remaining ~780 px shows one centred line. The list itself is cut
at the fold inside its own scroller. On the page where he has 58 things waiting, most of the screen shows nothing.
Fix (CSS): until a thread is open, let the list take the width in two columns or widen it to ~520 px and show the
preview text un-truncated; or fill the idle pane with the unread summary per channel (needs small logic).

**I2 [P2] Phone: channel tabs are cut with no cue; unread is one 9 px amber dot.**
The tab track ends mid-badge after WhatsApp (Telegram, Facebook, Instagram, কমেন্ট hidden, scrollbar hidden). Each row's
only unread signal is a 9 px amber dot at the far right plus a slightly bolder time.
Fix: same edge fade as Posts P5; make unread rows carry weight on the name (`font-weight:700` vs 500 for read) and
grow the dot to 10-12 px beside the name, not at the row end.

**I3 [P2] Preview lines show raw code.**
Mail previews contain literal `&#8202;`, `&zwnj;` and a stray "96" before the snippet on many rows. That is an escaping
fault in the preview text, not private content, and it makes real mail look like junk.
Fix: needs logic - decode HTML entities and strip zero-width / preheader padding before `esc()` in the thread-row
builder (or server side).

**I4 [P2] Top actions are unclear on the phone.**
"শব্দ বন্ধ" (`.bx-nt`) becomes a lone crossed-bell icon (its `.lb` label is `display:none`) next to a lone refresh icon;
"+ যুক্ত করুন" does not say what is added (a channel/account). The label "শব্দ বন্ধ" names the state, not what a press does.
Fix: keep the label on the phone (`.bx-nt .lb{display:inline}`), word it as state + switch look ("শব্দ: বন্ধ"), rename
the add button "+ চ্যানেল যুক্ত করুন".

**I5 [P3] "আরও দেখুন" at the foot of the list is bare grey text** - same door-as-text problem as Posts P3. Use `.bc-btn`.

## 4. Plan (`.pn-figs`, `.pn-adbox`/`.pn-adi`, `.pn-ctl`, `.pn-date`, `.pn-map.pf`, `.pf-chip`, `.pn-legend`, `.pf-flow`, `.pf-st`, `.pn-rules`, `.pn-line`)

**N1 [P1] The page opens on advertising detail, not on the schedule.**
Order today: mode sentence, three figures, then "সামনের ৭ দিনে ১৩টা বিজ্ঞাপনের প্রার্থী" as two six-row definition tables
(কখন / কোন পোস্ট / কিসের জন্য / কী ধরনের / কাকে দেখাবে / টাকা) + "আরও ১১টা দেখুন", and only then সময়সূচি. On the phone the schedule
starts ~1,300 px down. Ads are optional and cost money; the schedule is the page's job.
Fix (markup order): move the ad block under the schedule and fold it by default (it is already `details.pf-adbox`
inside the day view - reuse that pattern for the 7-day list). Show one line per candidate (network, when, purpose) with
the six rows behind the fold.

**N2 [P1] Schedule chips are too small to read or tap on the phone.**
`i.pf-chip` is 24 px tall with 11.5 px Bangla (the time), 4 px apart, ~50 px wide in a 58 px column; day sub-heads
(`.pn-dh em`) are 10.5 px. This is the main interactive object of the page and it is under both the 44 px touch rule
and the Bangla Size Rule. PC is tight but workable.
Fix: on the phone make the day head the tap target (it already selects the day) and say so; raise chips to
`height:30px;font-size:12.5px` under `@container bc (max-width:759px)` with `.pf-cell{gap:6px}`; `.pn-dh em{font-size:12px}`.
Default the phone to the "২ দিন" zoom so chips get width.

**N3 [P2] Seven-state legend, and a black status pill that looks like a button.**
The legend (`.pn-legend`) lists 7 states told apart by ring/fill/dash/dot at 18 px - too many to hold. In the day flow
"যাবে" is `.pf-st.q` = Ink fill, white text, the same paint as the dark action buttons, sitting right beside the real
button "পোস্ট দেখুন" (grey). Today's rule is "black is for actions"; here black is a status and grey is the action.
Fix: `.pf-st.q{background:#F2F3F5;color:var(--bc-ink);box-shadow:inset 0 0 0 1.5px var(--bc-ink)}`. Cut the legend to
the four states that need a decision (আপনার দেখা দরকার, ভিডিও লাগবে, ফাঁকা গেছে, বিজ্ঞাপনের প্রার্থী); the rest explain themselves
by the chip text.

**N4 [P2] Date control is a browser default in US format.**
`.pn-date input` shows "11/23/2026" in a Bangla page whose top bar says "সোমবার ২৩ নভেম্বর", beside a greyed "আজ" and two
bare arrows. DESIGN.md already describes a Bangla day picker for posts.
Fix: show the chosen date as Bangla text ("২৩ নভেম্বর") on a `.bc-btn` that opens the native picker
(`input.showPicker()`), or reuse the Bangla day select from the when-picker. Needs a few lines of script.

**N5 [P2] Three link styles for "show more" on one page.**
"পোস্টটা দেখুন", "দেখুন", "পরের ধাপের ঘরও দেখান", "রুটিনের ঘর বদলান" are underlined text (`.pn-link`); "আরও ১১টা দেখুন" is a
chevron fold (`.bc-fold`); "পোস্ট দেখুন" is a grey button. "প্ল্যান" / "প্ল্যান মিল ৪/৫" at the row end is a ghost button with
an icon. He cannot learn one "this opens something" look.
Fix: rule of thumb in CSS - opens a sheet or another page = `.bc-btn.sm`; unfolds in place = `.bc-fold`; drop
`.pn-link` except inside running sentences.

**N6 [P3] "১ নতুন লেখায় নিয়ম মানেনি" figure is amber like "৩২ লেখা আপনার অপেক্ষায়"** (`.pn-fg`), so two of three top tiles shout
equally. Keep amber for the one that needs his hand today; the rule mismatch can be the neutral tile with an amber dot.
The research list below ("গবেষণা মানা হচ্ছে কি না") is clear and well made - rows with a chevron open, rows without do not,
which is honest.

## 5. Ideas (`.bc-pills` tabs, `section.uc`, `.uc-i`, `.bc-nc`, `.uc-a`, `.tg-*`, `.ic-f`, `article.ic`, `.ic-p`, `.ic-a`, `.bc-steps`, `.bc-card-new`)

**D1 [P2] The second composer's icon is a "?" that is not help.**
`<span class="uc-i">?</span>` on "আইডিয়া থেকে পোস্ট" is a black square with a question mark. Everywhere else on the desk
(and by owner rule) "?" is the help button. He will press it for help and nothing happens.
Fix (markup): replace the literal "?" with the bulb icon already used in the rail (`svg('bulb')`/the Ideas icon).

**D2 [P2] Text-only buttons carry half the page's actions.**
"অ্যালবাম থেকে", "সময় ঠিক করুন", "জমা রাখুন", "এভাবে লেখা নিয়ে মন্তব্য", "আবার লিখুন", "মন্তব্য" are all `.bc-btn.ghost` - plain
words with no edge until hovered; on a phone there is no hover. "সময় ঠিক করুন" sits beside the grey sentence
"পরের ফাঁকা সময়ে যাবে" and reads as part of it. The first card has two dark buttons ("ফোন থেকে ছবি দিন", "পোস্ট লেখান") and no
"জমা রাখুন"; the second has one dark and "জমা রাখুন".
Fix (CSS): inside forms give ghost buttons an edge: `.uc .bc-btn.ghost,.ic-a .bc-btn.ghost{box-shadow:inset 0 0 0 1px var(--bc-rule)}`.
Make "ফোন থেকে ছবি দিন" the default grey `.bc-btn` so each card has one dark action. Add "জমা রাখুন" to the photo card or
remove it from both.

**D3 [P2] "কোথায় যাবে" chips: selected and not-selected are hard to tell.**
`.bc-nc.on` differs from off only by border colour and a 7 % tint; with the logo variant (`.bclg`) the ring dot is
hidden, so in the shots all four chips in both cards look the same. Where a post goes is the one choice he must not
get wrong. (I could not confirm from a still which are on - that is the finding.)
Fix (CSS): `.bc-nc.on{background:color-mix(in srgb,var(--c) 14%,#fff);box-shadow:inset 0 0 0 2px var(--c)}` plus a tick:
`.bc-nc.on::after{content:"✓";font-weight:700}`; off chips `opacity:.6`.

**D4 [P2] Idea cards are tall, tinted and repeat three weak actions.**
Each `article.ic` is ~290 px for one headline: tinted ground + coloured border + drop shadow (`.ic.has`), topic in a
new hue (purple, orange, green - DESIGN.md says non-network categories use the ink ramp), a row of 26 px network marks
(`.ic-p`, greyed at 45 % when unwritten - they look like disabled buttons but are only status), "২টা দেখুন", "আবার লিখুন",
then "মন্তব্য" floating under a gap. Twelve cards = 3,600 px on the phone before "আরও ৮৪টা আইডিয়া".
Fix (CSS): `article.ic{background:var(--bc-ground);border-color:var(--bc-rule)}`, `article.ic.has{box-shadow:none}`,
put `.ic-a` and the comment button on one row (`.ic-a{margin-top:8px}`; move the crit button inside `.ic-a`), and on
the phone clamp to a compact row (title + marks + one button). Label "২টা দেখুন" as "২টা লেখা দেখুন" - it does not say two
of what.

**D5 [P2] The board at the foot is an orphan.**
"বোর্ড ০": a seven-step line with a 105 px input ("নতুন কার্ড...") and an icon-only "+" (`.bc-card-new`). On the phone the
line runs off the right edge after the second step and the placeholder is cut. It is empty, unexplained, and sits
under 96 ideas.
Fix: when the board has no cards, fold it (`.bc-fold` "বোর্ড খুলুন") or hide it; `.bc-card-new .bc-in{min-width:220px;height:44px}`
and give the "+" a word ("যোগ করুন").

**D6 [P3] Tabs change shape between pages and widths.** Here the page tabs are small pills (13 px, ~30 px tall on PC)
while Posts/Inbox use the `big` size (14.5 px); on the phone the Ideas tabs are four separate grey pills wrapping to two
rows instead of one track. Use `.bc-pills.big` for every page-level tab row.

## 6. Media (album `.mw-i`/`.mw-p`/`.mw-more`, search, video library tiles with `.bc-row`)

**M1 [P1] The video library names every clip by file name.**
The lower grid shows "om-20261119-om-d203-d203.mp4", "om-20261118-om-d184-d184.mp4" (the same name three times) as the
bold title of each tile, cut with "..." on the phone. A trader cannot pick a clip by that; he has to play each one.
The search box above it invites words ("দোকানে কথা বলছি") that no title contains.
Fix: needs small logic - title = the clip's first on-screen line / hook / idea title if known, else "ভিডিও · ১৯ নভেম্বর · ০:৪৩";
keep the file name in `title=""` only. CSS stop-gap: `.lib tile b{display:none}` and let the date + duration lead.

**M2 [P2] One page, two libraries, no heading on the second.**
"অ্যালবাম ২৪টা ছবি, ৩৬টা ভিডিও" has a heading; after "আরও ৩৬টা দেখুন" a search field and a second video grid begin with no
title, different tile shape (16:9 vs 4:5 / 9:16), different actions ("Shorts বানান", "আইডিয়া বানান" vs "পোস্টে লাগান"). The
search looks like it searches the album above it.
Fix (markup): add a `.bc-h2` heading before the search ("লম্বা ভিডিও" or whatever these are in his words) with a count.

**M3 [P2] The amber ring on some album photos is unexplained.**
A 20 px amber ring sits top-left on some tiles; the caption says "ছবি · অপেক্ষায়" on the same tiles on PC, but on the phone
captions are hidden (`.mw-i:not(.big) figcaption{display:none}`), so the ring is the only sign and nothing says what
it means.
Fix: add a one-line legend beside the album count ("হলুদ রিং = অপেক্ষায় থাকা পোস্টে আছে") or replace the ring with a small
"অপেক্ষায়" tag on the photo.

**M4 [P2] Phone: album tiles show no action, and photo tiles in a row with a video leave white holes.**

## Design-specificity verdict

Authored, not generic - but less so than a week ago. What still belongs only to this product: network-as-line colour,
the time chips per network lane, the phone-post mirrors, the black "N টা লেখা আপনার অপেক্ষায়" bar, the Telegram-style idea
inbox, the "machine does this by itself" spine on Plan. Those could not be lifted into another product unchanged.
What has drifted toward category default: Home is now a seven-column kanban of tinted cards (the direction contract
explicitly refused "the old card stack"; the map lives only on Plan now), Ideas is a grid of same-size tinted cards,
People is a grid of same-size comment cards, Media is two thumbnail grids. Moving into the ERP shell was right for
navigation and it holds together well (same rail, same lime, white sheet on grey) - the cost is that DESIGN.md's Brand
Cockpit section now contradicts the build (it bans lime in the cockpit, says Ink for selected pills, says the map
leads Home). The document should be brought in line, or the next person will "fix" the wrong side.

## Cognitive load

Checklist failures: 4 of 8 -> high.
- **Single focus - fail.** Posts: the approve decision competes with 8 other controls per card. Plan: ads before schedule.
- **Chunking (<=4) - fail.** Post card action block 9 items; Plan legend 7 states; Inbox 7 channel tabs + 2 filters; Home status vocabulary 5.
- **Minimal choices - fail.** Same counts; plus two scores per post.
- **Progressive disclosure - fail on Home (past days), Ideas (12 full cards + empty board), People (79 comment cards);** pass on Posts (3 groups + jump strip), Media (24 tiles), Plan research list.
- Pass: grouping (idea groups under a rule, threads under their video), visual hierarchy on first screens (wait bar, page title, tabs), working memory (little carried between screens), one-thing-at-a-time in the composers.

Decision points with more than 4 visible options: post card (9), Plan schedule controls (5 zoom + date + 2 arrows + 7
network pills), Inbox tabs (7), idea card (network marks + 3 buttons), Listen header (4, one unlabelled).

## Emotional journey

Peak: the black wait bar with one big number and one amber button - it tells him exactly what he owes the desk, and the
Plan sentence "অনুমোদন ছাড়া কিছু যায় না" is real reassurance at the high-stakes moment. Valley: right after pressing that
button he lands on 32 cards with nine controls and two scores each; the confidence built on Home is spent in the first

board. No page tells him "আজকের কাজ শেষ".
