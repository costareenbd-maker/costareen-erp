# ERP critique - Assessment A4 (design review), 23 Nov 2026

Method: design review only (Assessment A, one reviewer, no detector). Evidence: 42 screenshots (21 screens x PC 1440x900 +
phone 390x844, Bangla mode, Clean look, demo shop), first viewport only, plus greps of `web/app.html` for names.
All 42 screenshots were present. Names marked "(confirm)" were not found by grep. Nothing below the first viewport,
no hover/focus/dark state and no interaction was checked.

Screens: mindmap, weborders, quotes, preorders, market, analytics, commission, capital, ai, brief, engine, watch,
skills, seo, brain, tools, syscheck, platform, apps, opentools, guide.

## Design specificity verdict

The money and sales screens here (capital, commission, analytics, web orders) read as this product: lime for the one
selection, pastel figure tiles, Bangla verb-first buttons. The owner-only AI and system screens (ai, brief, engine,
watch, skills, seo, brain, apps, opentools, platform) do not: each brings its own tab style, its own badge, its own help
affordance and partly its own language. They look like six small tools stitched under one header. The biggest
opportunity is not new design; it is making these ten screens use the components the first eleven already have.

## What is working

1. **Maintenance risk labelling.** Every job carries নিরাপদ or তথ্য বদলায় and the run button changes from Ink to soft
   red with it. That is exactly the reassurance an owner who is not a programmer needs before pressing.
2. **Phone reflow of tables.** Quotes and Analytics turn table rows into labelled cards with right-aligned Latin money;
   nothing scrolls sideways and figures stay comparable.
3. **Empty states that teach** on Pre-orders and Commission: one sentence saying what the thing is, and (commission) the
   button that starts it.

## Per-screen findings

### 1. mindmap (Navigator / ERP ম্যাপ)
- **P1 Node labels are unreadable and half English.** On PC the node titles render at about 6-7 px and sub-lines smaller
  ("Order sources", "Payment status", "Pre-orders & quo...", "Low or out", "Inventory &", "Orders, Invoice & Sales",
  "Customers with d..."); Bangla node text is heavy and clipped. The map is the screen's whole point and the owner cannot
  read it without zooming. Fix: raise the default fit zoom so node titles are at least 12.5 px at 1440 wide, and pass the
  node labels through the dictionary (there is already `'Orders, Invoice & Sales': 'অর্ডার, ইনভয়েস ও বিক্রি'`; the canvas
  text is not using it). Needs logic in the `nvg` / `nav*` block (`navDetails`, node render) (confirm function name).
- **P2 Violet selected states.** The filter chip "এ মাসে" has a violet ring and the details panel tab "ওভারভিউ" is a violet
  pill with violet figures (৳0). DESIGN.md: never violet for selected. Fix: in the `nvg-` tab and chip rules use
  `--fd-surface-3` + Ink 700 + 1px firm hairline (neutral selected); figures in Ink.
- **P2 Two period controls.** Lime chips "এই মাস / ৩ মাস / ..." and, one row lower, a second "এ মাসে" dropdown chip. Which
  one rules? Fix: remove the dropdown chip from the filter row or bind it to the chips and hide it.
- **P2 Phone: the map is below six KPI cards.** First viewport on phone is title, 5 period chips, search, filters and four
  white KPI cards; the map is two screens down. Fix: on <=760px collapse the KPI cards into the "সারাংশ ৪টি সংখ্যা" fold
  that weborders/quotes already use.
- **P3** Breadcrumb says নেভিগেটর, H1 says ERP ম্যাপ, side menu lights ড্যাশবোর্ড: three names for one screen. KPI cards
  here are white with borders, not the pastel tiles used everywhere else.

### 2. weborders
- **P2 Empty state ignores the real cause.** The chip says "ওয়েবসাইট: যুক্ত নেই" and the card says "এখনও ওয়েবসাইটের কোনো
  অর্ডার আনা হয়নি" with a primary "ওয়েবসাইটে নতুন অর্ডার দেখুন" that cannot work while not connected. Fix (small logic):
  when not connected, the empty text becomes "ওয়েবসাইট এখনো যুক্ত নেই" and the primary becomes "ওয়েবসাইট যুক্ত করুন"
  (to Settings > Website); disable the fetch button.
- **P3 Status chip glued to the card.** "ওয়েবসাইট: যুক্ত নেই" sits ~4 px above the card while every other gap is 16-20 px.
  Fix: `margin-bottom:12px` on the chip wrapper (confirm class).
- **P3** "ওয়েবসাইটের প্রোডাক্ট মেলান ✓": the tick reads as "done". Drop the glyph. Third KPI tile wraps its label to two
  lines so its figure sits 4 px higher than its neighbours; shorten to "এ মাসের ওয়েব অর্ডার".

### 3. quotes
- **P2 Two KPI rows say the same thing.** Row 1: এ মাসের কোটেশন 1, দাম, তৈরি-ইনভয়েস হয়নি 0, খসড়া 0. Row 2 (under the
  sub-tabs): কোটেশন 1, খসড়া 0, প্রস্তুত 0, ইনভয়েস হয়েছে 1. খসড়া appears twice. On PC it costs 190 px before the list.
  Fix: drop the second row on the ড্যাশবোর্ড sub-tab (or make row 1 the status counts); markup only.
- **P2 English "title" in the table head** ("কাস্টমার · title", phone "কাস্টমার · TITLE"). Source `<th>Customer · title</th>`
  in the quotes list; add the dictionary entry `'Customer · title': 'কাস্টমার · শিরোনাম'`.
- **P2 Codes wrap.** "QUO-2026-00001" and "INV-20261110-1189" break over two lines on a 1440 screen while the title
  column has spare width. Fix: `white-space:nowrap` on the reference cell and the invoice link.
- **P3** Sub-tab named "ড্যাশবোর্ড" clashes with the main ড্যাশবোর্ড; call it "তালিকা". Phone: "+ নতুন কোটেশন"
  (`#qz_newbtn2` family) sits inside the filter card under রিফ্রেশ; fine, but 36 px tall.

### 4. preorders
- **P2 Phone: the selected tab is off-screen.** The chip row shows সব ইনভয়েস / ওয়েব অর্ডার / কোটেশন; the lime
  "প্রি-অর্ডার" is scrolled out, so nothing on screen is selected (quotes does scroll its chip into view). Fix (2 lines
  of logic): after paint, `scrollIntoView({inline:'center',block:'nearest'})` on the selected chip.
- **P2 KPI tiles are mis-built.** The icon square floats mid-left between figure and label, the figure is indented past
  it, and the tile is 150 px tall with dead space (phone: "৳0" tile has 40 px gaps). They are `fdhCard` tiles in
  `.fdd-grid` with `.fdd-k{min-height:176px}`; every other screen uses the compact icon-left tile. Fix: use the same
  compact KPI builder as weborders/quotes, or set `.fdd-k{min-height:0}` and put icon and figure in one row.
- **P3** No H1 and no summary line (siblings have both); a lone "সাহায্য" button sits under the tabs. "মাল স্টকে আসার আগেই"
  and "মাল দেওয়া পর্যন্ত": glossary word is প্রোডাক্ট.

### 5. market
- **P2 English sentence in the Bangla intro:** "Suppliers are shown by city only until they give you a price." The
  dictionary key is the whole sentence with the "You see the platform's own stock..." prefix, so this variant misses.
  Fix: split the string in the market block so the last sentence has its own key.
- **P2 The selected tab is a black button, beside a black "খুঁজুন".** `.mk-tabs .btn.primary` marks the tab; two Ink
  fills in one card and lime is unused. Fix: make `.mk-tabs` buttons chips; selected = lime (this is the screen's one
  selection row); only "খুঁজুন" stays Ink.
- **P2 Weak empty state.** "মার্কেটে এখনও কিছু নেই।" with no next step, although the intro's own advice is to share stock.
  Fix: add the "আপনার স্টক দেখান" button under it (it is only bold text in the intro today).
- **P3** "মার্কেট" three times (breadcrumb, H1, card title); drop the card title.

### 6. analytics
- **P1 Status column says "খুলুন" (the verb "open it") for an open month.** Source: `'<span class="fd22-st warn"><i></i>' +
  (r.current ? 'Open · to date' : 'Open')` falls into the generic `'Open': 'খুলুন'`. On an amber pill it looks like a
  button and tells the owner nothing about whether the month is closed. Fix: emit `'Open (month)'` or a dedicated key
  and translate "খোলা"; closed = "বন্ধ".
- **P2 English paragraphs in Bangla mode.** "Month by month, the Profitability screen's own figures..." (`FD22_NOTE.pnl`)
  and the card sub "Revenue, cost of goods, gross, expenses and net..." (`fd22TCard` sub). Add both to the dictionary
  (and the other `FD22_NOTE` entries: growth, roi...).
- **P3 Empty card body.** "মাসিক লাভ" card is a header, a hairline and 30 px of nothing (`body: ''`). Fix: no hairline
  when body is empty, or fold the intro into the table card.
- **P3** "মালের কেনা দাম" -> "কেনা দাম" (glossary). Two tab systems stacked (underline tabs, then chips).

### 7. commission
- **P2 Raw month code.** "2026-11" in the month picker, the card title "বিক্রেতা · 2026-11" and the empty headline
  "2026-11-এ কোনো কমিশন হয়নি". Rule: dates in Bangla ("নভেম্বর ২০২৬"). Fix: format `d.month` with the month-label helper
  before it reaches `f21Empty(...)` and the card title.
- **P2 Two primaries for one action.** "+ নিয়ম" (top right, Ink) and "নিয়ম যোগ করুন" (`data-cmsrule`, Ink) in the empty
  state. On phone "+ নিয়ম" is a full-width black bar above the tabs. Fix: when there are no rules, render the header
  button as secondary (`.btn` not `.btn.primary`); on phone keep it auto-width.
- **P3** Phone: ~40 px gap between the chip row and the KPI tiles, double the gap elsewhere. Tiles 1 and 2 are both
  lavender (cycle should start peach).

### 8. capital
- **P2 Two words for one idea.** Tab "ইনভেস্টর", button "ইনভেস্টর যোগ করুন", chip "ইনভেস্টর" against tile "বিনিয়োগকারীদের
  কাছে দেনা", "মোট বিনিয়োগ". Glossary: one word per idea. Pick ইনভেস্টর (staff word) and change the tile label.
- **P3** Ownership bar is warning-amber for a healthy 100% owner; use Ink or a neutral series colour. "100 %" has a
  space and a small grey %; "লেখা শেয়ার" is unclear ("শেয়ার লেখা হয়েছে"). Three header buttons are fine (one Ink).

### 9. ai (অপেক্ষায়)
- **P2 "AI কর্মী এখন বন্ধ" has no way out.** The `.nai-off` banner (repeated on ai, brief, engine, watch, skills, seo,
  brain) states the problem and stops. The owner cannot start it or learn who can. Fix: add one line + button inside
  `.nai-off` ("কীভাবে চালু করবেন" opening the help entry, or "চালু করুন" where the owner can); show the full banner once
  on ai and a one-line version on the sub-screens.
- **P3** No breadcrumb/H1 here although the six sibling AI screens have one. Grey explainer under the buttons
  ("এখনই জিজ্ঞেস করুন: ... ইঞ্জিন: ...") duplicates the "?" beside the label; owner rule says help goes behind "?".
  "OK" inside Bangla ("আপনার OK-র অপেক্ষায়"); "আপনার হ্যাঁ-র অপেক্ষায়" or "অনুমোদনের অপেক্ষায়".

### 10. brief

- **P3** "প্রতি সন্ধ্যা 20:00-এ" should be "প্রতি রাত ৮টায়" (times in Bangla digits). Screen is otherwise clean: one

### 14. seo

### 15. brain
