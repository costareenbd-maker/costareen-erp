## Direction contract (Brand Cockpit, owner picked Line Map 13 Nov 2026; PC first, phone second)

THESIS: The week is a metro map. Every network is a coloured line, every post a station (gone / queued / stuck), and a black "today" rule cuts the map. Refuses the category default of KPI cards on top and a table below; refuses the old ERP card stack and its tab chips.

OWN-WORLD: White ground #FFFFFF, ink #16181D, a cool second neutral #F2F3F5 for the rail and panels, 1px rules #E4E6EA. Colour only comes from the network lines (LinkedIn #0A66C2, Facebook #1877F2, Instagram #C13584, YouTube #E62117, TikTok #111, Bluesky #1185FE, Telegram #229ED9), from stuck red #E0301E and from waiting amber #F2A900. Stations are 19px rings; queued stations are filled; stuck stations are red with a cross. Pill tabs, black bars for what waits for him, Pretendard + Hind Siliguri, tabular numbers.

STORY: He opens one place, sees the whole week without entering any tool, approves what waits, fixes what is stuck, and sees what came back (people, comments, leads). The tools behind it stay hidden; a Connections map shows how they hang together, live.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop 1440: left rail 232px (sections with live counts); centre the black "N posts wait for you" bar, then the line map at full width (7 day columns, post title on hover, click a station to open it), under it a stuck callout and three result tiles; right column 400px holds the waiting drafts with Approve inline. Phone 390: rail becomes a bottom bar, the map scrolls sideways, drafts open under the bar.

FORM: Line Map, impeccable's pick (position 2 on the ordered list), seed:7e4abafe29a0cde7. Signature interaction: hover a station lights its whole line and names the post; clicking it opens the post beside the map.
