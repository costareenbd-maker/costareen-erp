# Shape: Brand desk schedule, plan fit, 60-day plans (brand-fit-1, 17 Nov 2026)

## Owner's ask (his words, condensed)
- A button beside every post, picture and video that shows what the writing keeps of our plan and research, which skills
  the picture/video went through, and how it lines up with the plan - so he can rely on every piece.
- Each day of the schedule: is any post an ad candidate, and what kind of ad - shown simply and nicely.
- The 60-day platform plans must live in the system; the next 60 days are planned from them on the same research.
- The schedule / routine looks messy, boring, tiring. He wants to see 1 day, 2 days, one date, up to a month; zoom in and
  out, or drag sideways at least 30 days ahead, or shrink to see 7-14 days at once.
- Use impeccable fully: build, audit, review, rebuild.

## Decisions (Claude decides; owner delegated)
- Mode: Operate. World: Brand Cockpit Line Map (no new hues). Colour only on network lines, waiting amber, stuck red.
- Schedule = the Line Map extended: networks are lines, posts are stations at their exact time, 35 days rendered,
  zoom pills 1 / 2 / 7 / 14 / 30 days set the column width, the canvas scrolls and drags sideways (mouse drag, wheel,
  touch). Today rule in Ink. Day heads carry an ink-outline ad count. Clicking a day or station opens that day's
  departures below, with an ad box that says in plain words what ad would run and when money is spent.
- Plan fit = a sheet on the comment-sheet shell: a score (rules kept / rules), the plan slot (time, format, series,
  language, cycle day), every research rule on this draft, the steps it went through as an Ink spine (idea, writer
  with its inputs, editor, claim check + guard, picture/video pipeline with the skills used), its ad role.
- ৬০ দিন page: cycle bar (day N of 60, weeks), per-network 60-day table straight from the blueprints (current week
  row shaded), and how the next cycle is written (day 50, same research + measured results, his yes before it runs).
- Machine: bridge /plan?days&start, /fit, /cycles, cycle_write at day 50 (drafts in /data/brand/cards/_cycles).
