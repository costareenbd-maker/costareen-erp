Privacy Policy

Rahman Najir Social Desk · last updated 22 November 2026

"Rahman Najir Social Desk" is a private tool used only by Abidur Rahman Nazir ("Rahman Najir") to publish posts and answer comments and messages on the Facebook and Instagram Pages he manages himself. It is not offered to the public and no one else signs in to it.

What the tool reads

  • The list of Pages he manages and the access the Page owner granted (to publish on them).
  • Comments and messages that people send to those Pages, so that he can read and answer them.
  • Basic public profile details Meta passes with a comment or message (name, profile id), and post insights (reach, reactions) of his own posts.

What it does with it

  • Publishes the posts he approves to his own Pages.
  • Shows him incoming comments and messages and sends the replies he approves.
  • Counts how his own posts perform, to plan the next posts.

The data is never sold, rented or shared with advertisers or any other third party, and it is not used to build profiles of people. It is kept on servers he controls and only for as long as it is needed to answer a conversation or measure a post (at most 12 months), then deleted.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can ask for any data about you (your comments, messages and profile details received through this tool) to be deleted. See Data deletion instructions, or write to abidur@rmabidur.com. Requests are completed within 30 days.
