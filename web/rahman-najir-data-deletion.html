Data deletion instructions

Rahman Najir Social Desk · last updated 22 November 2026

Rahman Najir Social Desk keeps only the comments, messages and basic profile details (name, profile id) that people send to Abidur Rahman Nazir's own Facebook and Instagram Pages, so that he can answer them.

How to have your data deleted

  1. Send an email to abidur@rmabidur.com with the subject "Data deletion request", or send the same words as a message to the Rahman Najir Facebook Page.
  2. Tell us the name or profile you used to comment or message.
  3. Everything stored about you by this tool is deleted within 30 days and you get a confirmation reply.

Removing the app's access

If you are a Page admin who connected a Page to this tool, you can also remove its access at any time in Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations → Rahman Najir Social Desk → Remove.
