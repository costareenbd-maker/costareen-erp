Privacy Policy

Costareen ERP (including the "Costareen ERP Inbox" app) · Last updated 15 October 2026

Costareen ERP is the business software used by Costareen, Dhaka, Bangladesh, to run its shop: sales, stock and customer messages. This policy explains what we do with information when a customer writes to one of our Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts, WhatsApp numbers or our website chat.

What we receive

  • The messages you send to our Page or account, and our replies.
  • Your public name and, where the platform shares it, your profile picture, as given to us by Meta.
  • Details you choose to give us in a chat to place an order: name, phone number, delivery address.
  • Comments you leave on our own posts.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions, show product details and prices, and take and deliver your orders.
  • Replies may be written by our staff or by an AI assistant that uses only our product list and your conversation.
  • To keep a record of orders and payments, as any shop does.

We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising profiles, and we do not share it with anyone except the delivery company that carries your parcel (name, phone, address) and the service providers that run our systems.

What we keep

We keep the text of the conversation and order details. Pictures, voice notes, videos and profile pictures are not copied to our servers: they stay with Meta and are shown to our staff from there. If you delete a message on Facebook or Instagram, it is no longer shown to us.

Where it is kept

On our database provider (Supabase, Singapore region) and our web host (Cloudflare). Access is limited to Costareen staff with their own accounts.

Your choices

  • You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it: see Data deletion.
  • You can stop our messages at any time by writing STOP.
