Data deletion instructions

Costareen ERP (including the "Costareen ERP Inbox" app)

If you wrote to a Costareen Facebook Page, Instagram account, WhatsApp number or website chat and want us to delete what we hold about you:

  1. Send a message to the same Page or account saying "Delete my data", or e-mail costareen.bd@gmail.com with the name and phone number or Facebook name you used.
  2. We confirm the request within 3 working days and delete your conversations, contact details and chat history from Costareen ERP within 30 days.
  3. Records the law requires a shop to keep (invoices and payments for goods you bought) are kept without your chat history, only as long as required.

Removing the app from your Facebook account

If you connected Costareen ERP Inbox to your own Facebook account (business owners only), open Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, choose Costareen ERP Inbox and press Remove. The keys we held for your Pages stop working at once; tell us at the address above and we delete them from our system.
